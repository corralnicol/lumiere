Are you ready to begin?

Step 1: Choose a sales plan

We offer two sales plans, so you can choose the right package of tools and services at the right price.

The Individual sales plan costs $1.00 per sale.

The Professional sales plan costs $39.99 per month, regardless of how many items you sell.

You can modify or cancel your sales plan at any time.

Lumiere also charges a referral fee for each product you sell, and in some cases, other selling fees may apply. Some optional tools and programs involve additional costs.

Step 2: Create your Lumiere seller account

Visit Lumiere.com and click Sign Up. Then, create the account using the email address associated with your Lumiere customer account or a separate email address from your business.

Before creating your seller account, make sure you have the following:

  • Bank account and routing number
  • Credit card for international payments
  • Government-issued ID
  • Tax information
  • Phone number

See our beginner's overview for step-by-step instructions on creating an Lumiere seller account.

Step 3: Start selling

After creating an Lumiere seller account, you'll have access to Seller Control and can set up your public seller profile, shipping and returns, tax, payment, and business information.

Then you can list and price your products, register your brand, choose a fulfillment method, and take advantage of tools and programs to promote products and connect with customers.
